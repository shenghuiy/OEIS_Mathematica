# A064174: partitions of n with nonnegative rank

`src/wolfram/A064174.wl` is a Wolfram Language package (context `OEIS`) for [A064174](https://oeis.org/A064174), the number of partitions of n with nonnegative rank. The rank of a partition is its largest part minus its number of parts (Dyson). Equivalently, a(n) counts the partitions whose largest part is at least the number of parts, or, by conjugation, at most the number of parts. The complement, partitions with negative rank, is [A064173](https://oeis.org/A064173).

**Notation.** $N(m, n)$ is the number of partitions of $n$ with rank $m$ (see [A101198](A101198.md)), and $p(n)$ the number of all partitions of $n$, `PartitionsP[n]` in Wolfram.

## The sequence

The terms for $n = 1, 2, 3, \dots$ begin

```
1, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 17, 23, 31, 42, 56, 73, 96, 125, 161, 207, 265, 336, ...
```

For example $a(8) = 12$: of the 22 partitions of 8, the 12 with largest part at least the number of parts are 8, 7+1, 6+2, 6+1+1, 5+3, 5+2+1, 5+1+1+1, 4+4, 4+3+1, 4+2+2, 4+2+1+1 and 3+3+2.

## Public functions

| Call | Returns |
|---|---|
| `A064174[n]` | The number of partitions of $n$ with nonnegative rank, as a sum of partition numbers. |
| `A064174List[nmax]` | $\{a(1), \dots, a(n_{\max})\}$, from a recurrence in the sequence itself. |

`n` and `nmax` are positive integers. Otherwise the function gives a message (`::posint`) and returns `$Failed`. Like the OEIS entry, the sequence starts at $n = 1$, so the empty partition is not counted.

## How it works

1. **The generating function.** Dyson's rank generating function, equation (2.12) of Atkin and Swinnerton-Dyer (1954) and derived on the [A101198](A101198.md) page, gives for each $m \ge 0$

   $$\sum_{n \ge 1} N(m, n)\, x^n = \frac{\sum_{k \ge 1} (-1)^{k-1} x^{k(3k-1)/2 + mk} (1 - x^k)}{\prod_{r \ge 1} (1 - x^r)}$$

   Summing over $m \ge 0$, $\sum_{m \ge 0} x^{mk} = 1/(1 - x^k)$ cancels the factor $1 - x^k$:

   $$\sum_{n \ge 1} a(n)\, x^n = \frac{\sum_{k \ge 1} (-1)^{k-1} x^{k(3k-1)/2}}{\prod_{r \ge 1} (1 - x^r)}$$

   This is the generating function in the OEIS entry (Manyama, 2023).
2. **One term.** Expanding $1/\prod (1 - x^r) = \sum p(j)\, x^j$:

   $$a(n) = \sum_{k \ge 1} (-1)^{k-1}\, p\!\left(n - \tfrac{k(3k-1)}{2}\right) = p(n-1) - p(n-5) + p(n-12) - p(n-22) + \cdots$$

   with $p(j) = 0$ for $j < 0$ (Jovovic, 2004). The sum has about $\sqrt{2n/3}$ terms. This is `A064174[n]`.
3. **Euler's recurrence split by the sign of the rank.** Euler's pentagonal recurrence $p(n) = \sum_{k \ge 1} (-1)^{k-1} \left[ p\!\left(n - \tfrac{k(3k-1)}{2}\right) + p\!\left(n - \tfrac{k(3k+1)}{2}\right) \right]$ has two halves. The first half is $a(n)$, so the second is the count of partitions with negative rank:

   $$\text{A064173}(n) = p(n) - a(n) = \sum_{k \ge 1} (-1)^{k-1}\, p\!\left(n - \tfrac{k(3k+1)}{2}\right)$$

   Their difference is $N(0, n)$ ([A047993](https://oeis.org/A047993)), and since conjugation makes the counts for rank $> 0$ and rank $< 0$ equal, $a(n) = \bigl(p(n) + N(0, n)\bigr)/2$.
4. **A recurrence without partition numbers.** Multiplying the generating function by $\prod_{r \ge 1} (1 - x^r) = 1 + \sum_{k \ge 1} (-1)^k \left( x^{k(3k-1)/2} + x^{k(3k+1)/2} \right)$ and comparing coefficients of $x^n$:

   $$a(n) = \sum_{k \ge 1} (-1)^{k-1} \left[ a\!\left(n - \tfrac{k(3k-1)}{2}\right) + a\!\left(n - \tfrac{k(3k+1)}{2}\right) \right] + \varepsilon(n)$$

   with $a(j) = 0$ for $j \le 0$, and $\varepsilon(n) = (-1)^{k-1}$ if $n = k(3k-1)/2$ for some $k \ge 1$, otherwise $0$. So the correction is $+1, -1, +1, -1, \dots$ at $n = 1, 5, 12, 22, \dots$. This is the same recurrence as Euler's for $p(n)$, but with $\varepsilon(n)$ in place of $[n = 0]$. `A064174List` runs it: each term adds about $\sqrt{8n/3}$ earlier terms, so the whole list up to $n_{\max} = 2000$ takes about 0.16 s, against about 5.8 s for a `Table` of the one-term sums.
5. **Asymptotics.** From $a(n) = \bigl(p(n) + N(0, n)\bigr)/2$, the expansion $p(n) \sim \dfrac{e^{\pi\sqrt{2n/3}}}{4\sqrt{3}\, n}\left(1 - \dfrac{\sqrt{3/2}/\pi + \pi/(24\sqrt{6})}{\sqrt{n}}\right)$ and $N(0, n) \sim \dfrac{\pi\, e^{\pi\sqrt{2n/3}}}{3 \cdot 2^{9/2}\, n^{3/2}}$ (the same leading term as for rank 1),

   $$a(n) \sim \frac{e^{\pi\sqrt{2n/3}}}{8\sqrt{3}\, n} \left(1 + \frac{c}{\sqrt{n}}\right), \qquad c = \frac{5\pi}{24\sqrt{6}} - \frac{\sqrt{6}}{2\pi} \approx -0.12265$$

   The ratio of $a(n)$ to the leading term is 0.9875, 0.9961 and 0.9988 at $n = 100$, $1000$ and $10000$, and $\sqrt{n}\,\bigl(a(n)/\text{leading term} - 1\bigr)$ is $-0.12327$ and $-0.12285$ at $n = 1000$ and $10000$. This constant was checked numerically, not proved here.

The recurrence in step 4 and the asymptotic in step 5 are not in the OEIS entry as of October 2026.

## Checks

Run `test/wolfram/A064174.wlt`. It checks:

- the examples on this page;
- the DATA section of the OEIS entry, and values from its b-file at $n = 100$, $500$, $1000$, $5000$ (list) and $10000$ (single term);
- the list against brute force with `IntegerPartitions` for $n \le 30$, using the definition and both other descriptions in the OEIS comments (largest part at most the number of parts; Kimberling's $\max(\text{largest part}, \text{number of parts})$ is a part);
- the list (recurrence) against the single-term sums for $n \le 600$;
- $p(n) - a(n)$ against the sum over $k(3k+1)/2$ and against brute force (A064173), and $2a(n) - p(n)$ against the rank-0 sum and the DATA of A047993;
- the series expansion of the generating function, and of Paul D. Hanna's generating function $\sum_{m \ge 1} x^m \prod_{k=1}^{m} \frac{1 - x^{m+k-1}}{1 - x^k}$ from the OEIS entry;
- the asymptotic and its constant $c$ at $n = 10000$;
- the messages for invalid arguments.
